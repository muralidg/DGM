IT guidance

Field notes from our engineers

NETWORK

Five signs your office network is overdue for an upgrade

Slow file transfers, Wi-Fi dead spots, routers restarted every week, one flat network for everything, and cables nobody has labelled.

BACKUP

A backup you have never restored is only a hope

Run a restore test every quarter and keep at least one copy offsite or in the cloud.

EMAIL

Before you move to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace

Audit mailboxes, plan the DNS changes, switch on multi-factor sign-in on day one, and migrate in batches.

CCTV

Sizing CCTV storage the right way

Storage depends on camera count, resolution, recording mode and how many days of footage you need to keep.
